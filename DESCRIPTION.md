# LFG Lens — Store listing description

**"LFM ST" — which ST?**

Forever's new Shaper's Terrace just stole the Sunken Temple's acronym. Barrow Deeps wants BD from Blackrock Depths. DM still means two dungeons. And with no dungeon finder, all of this is happening in scrolling public chat, in launch week, while nobody's slang has settled yet.

LFG Lens fixes the reading, not the players:

- Every dungeon acronym in channel chat becomes a link. **Hover** for the full name, level range, entrance, and — when an acronym can mean two dungeons — *both meanings, flagged*.
- **Click** to pin the post into the Lens window: the last 20 dungeon posts with age stamps, filters for New in Forever / Classic / My level, and click-a-row to whisper the poster.
- The shipped dictionary covers 33 dungeons and raids — all nine Forever-new dungeons, the December raids, and the classic set — and it knows its limits: unpublished level ranges say "not yet published," and new-dungeon acronyms are labelled *proposals*.
- When your server settles on different slang, `/lfglens alias` repoints any acronym in seconds. Your word wins.

Tiny, free, open source (MIT), no libraries, no addon comms, no auto-anything. Forever only (interface 16001).

*Pre-launch note: v0.1.0 is built from beta-era reporting and is awaiting verification in the live client after the November 4, 2026 launch.*
